Why "404"?

In Locked 2, every match comes down to marking, timing, and who cracks first under pressure — a missed tackle, a blown mark, a lost header. We named ourselves after the one error message we refuse to become. Every match, every set piece, every last-minute press — we treat it like a bug to patch, not a wall to fear.

Founded by EU players who kept carrying matches solo and decided it was time to stop doing it alone. THE 404 TEAM is built around calm marking, fast build-up play, and knowing exactly when to press.
